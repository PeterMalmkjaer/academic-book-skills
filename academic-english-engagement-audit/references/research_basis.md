# Research basis — academic-english-engagement-audit

Confidence-tagged. **[verified]** checked against a primary source during build;
**[classic]** established work cited from disciplinary knowledge.

## The validated core: Human Interest
- **Flesch, R. (1948/1949). *The Art of Readable Writing* / "A new readability yardstick",
  J. Applied Psychology 32(3), 221–233. [verified]** Defines the **Human-Interest**
  formula: HI = 3.635·(personal words per 100 words) + 0.314·(personal sentences per 100
  sentences). *Personal words* = personal pronouns, gendered nouns/names, "people/folks";
  *personal sentences* = quoted speech, questions, commands/requests, direct address,
  exclamations, incomplete sentences. Bands: ~30 interesting, 50 very interesting, 80
  dramatic. This is the skill's headline, validated number.

## Text features that drive reading interest (proxies)
- **Concreteness / imageability is the strongest text-based interest predictor**
  (Sadoski, Goetz & Fritz, 1993, J. Educ. Psychol. 85; Sadoski, Goetz & Rodriguez, 2000,
  JEP 92; dual-coding theory, Paivio). [verified] Concrete texts are recalled better and
  rated more interesting; concreteness was "overwhelmingly the best predictor of overall
  comprehensibility, interest, and recall." → concreteness proxy.
- **Situational interest is triggered by coherence, vividness, character identification,
  suspense, novelty/unexpectedness** (Schraw & Lehman, 2001, Educ. Psychol. Rev. 13;
  Schraw, 1997; Hidi & Baird, 1988). [verified] Ease of comprehension and vividness
  explained ~45% of variance in perceived interest. → engagement + narrativity proxies.
- **Reading motivation / interest development** (Hidi & Renninger, 2006; Guthrie et al.,
  2006, RRQ). [verified] Situational interest feeds engagement and long-term motivation.
- **Narrative aids engagement/comprehension for non-experts** (Dahlstrom, 2014, PNAS).
  [classic]

## The mandatory caveat: seductive details
- **Harp & Mayer (1998), "How seductive details do their damage", JEP 90(3), 414–434;
  Garner et al. (1989); meta-analysis: Sundararajan & Adesope (2020), Educ. Psychol.
  Rev. 32. [verified]** Interesting-but-**irrelevant** details *reduce* recall and
  transfer by priming the wrong schema. Sadoski: concrete elaboration of *important* ideas
  helps, but adding enough irrelevant interest that a new coherence emerges harms learning.
  → **The score rewards relevant concreteness and coherence, never decorative interest.**
  A recommendation to "raise engagement" must never mean "add vivid trivia."

## Design consequences
1. HI is validated; the other components are **evidence-informed proxies** — reported
   separately and labelled as such. 2. Engagement ≠ readability ≠ absence of AI tells.
3. Genre-aware: dense expository/theory prose legitimately scores low HI. 4. Measure only;
   rewriting (fidelity-bound) belongs to the register skill. 5. Seductive-details guardrail
   is non-negotiable.
